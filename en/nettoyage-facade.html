Facade cleaning

Clean without damaging, protect without suffocating.

Moss, lichen, pollution and streaks blacken facades and weaken materials. Yet poorly controlled cleaning can damage soft stone such as tufa or old renders.

We adapt the method to each surface: biocide treatment, low-pressure rinse, then a breathable water repellent that limits the return of dirt.

  • Anti-moss and anti-lichen treatment
  • Low-pressure cleaning suited to the surface
  • Breathable colourless water repellent
  • Respect for soft stone and old renders

Frequently asked questions

You may be wondering…

Another question? Call Gad directly on 07 68 78 92 58.

Does high-pressure cleaning damage the facade?

On soft surfaces, yes. That is why we prefer a chemical treatment followed by a low-pressure rinse.

How long does a water-repellent treatment last?

Depending on exposure, a quality water repellent protects the facade for 5 to 10 years.

Should the facade be cleaned before repainting?

Always. Paint applied to a dirty or mossy surface will not adhere for long.
