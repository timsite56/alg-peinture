Our area

The Mauges: village houses and stone longères

From Beaupréau to Andrezé, from Jallais to La Chapelle-du-Genêt, the villages of the Mauges have many stone houses and renovated longères. Their old walls need breathable paints and patient surface preparation.

Interiors with exposed beams and wooden staircases are ideal for our lacquers and woodwork renovations, and old parquet floors sand and varnish beautifully.
