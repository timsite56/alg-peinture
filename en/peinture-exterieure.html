Exterior painting

A facade that stands the test of the seasons.

Exterior painting is first and foremost a protection. Sun, rain, frost and pollution attack surfaces: the choice of product and the preparation determine how long the work will last.

We use breathable, pliolite or siloxane paints depending on the surface, and adapt each system to the climate of the region where your property is located.

  • Surface assessment and choice of system
  • Breathable and siloxane paints
  • Scaffolding and site protection
  • Compliance with colours approved by the town hall

Frequently asked questions

Another question? Call Gad directly on 07 68 78 92 58.

When is the best time to paint a facade?

From spring to autumn, in dry weather with temperatures between 8 and 30 °C. We schedule the work according to the weather.

Do I need permission to repaint my facade?

A change of colour often requires a prior declaration to the town hall. We will help you with this process.

How long does a facade paint job last?

With good preparation and the right product, allow 10 to 15 years depending on exposure.

Do you work throughout Western France?

Yes. Based in Saint-Pierre-Montlimart, we carry out exterior projects throughout Western France.
